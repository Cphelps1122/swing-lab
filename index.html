No pitches yet

Film pitches from behind home plate or behind the pitcher and Swing Lab measures velocity, movement and spin.

  1. Put the phone on a tripod behind the plate (behind the catcher or backstop) or behind the pitcher, centered and level. Turn on the camera's grid/level.
  2. Use the Camera app's Slo-Mo mode (240 fps if you can). Keep the whole flight, hand to glove, in the frame.
  3. Tap Add pitch, pick the clip and the pitch type, and keep the screen open.

Velo = release speed (mph). Vert = vertical break from spin, inches (+ rise, − drop). Horz = horizontal break, inches (+ toward the pitcher's arm side). Spin is experimental. Tap a pitcher for her arsenal and movement chart.
